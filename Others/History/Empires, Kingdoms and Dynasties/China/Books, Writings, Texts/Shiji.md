# Shiji

The Shiji (traditional Chinese: 史記; lit. Historical Records), also known as Records of the Grand Historian or The Grand Scribe's Records (traditional Chinese: 太史公書), is a historical text written c. 91 BCE by [[Sima Qian]]. The work covers a 2,500-year period from the age of the legendary [[Yellow Emperor]] to the reign of Emperor Wu of Han in the author's own time, and describes the world as it was known to the Chinese of The Occidental Han Dynasty.
