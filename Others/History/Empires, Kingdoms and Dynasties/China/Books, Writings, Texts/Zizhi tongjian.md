# Zizhi tongjian

Zizhi tongjian (Chinese: 資治通鑑, lit. "Comprehensive Mirror to Aid in Government") is a chronicle published in 1084 CE during the Northern Song Dynasty. It provides a record of Chinese history from 403 BCE to 959 CE, covering 16 dynasties and spanning almost 1400 years.
