# Shaohao

Shaohao (Chinese: 少皞), also known as Jin Tian (Chinese: 金天), was a legendary Chinese sovereign, commonly identified as a son of the [[Yellow Emperor]].

According to some traditions, such as that within the [[Book of Documents]], the [[Zizhi tongjian]] and the Sovereign Series, Shaohao is one of the legendary [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors]].

His predecessor is [[Yellow Emperor]] while his sucessor is [[Zhuanxu]].
