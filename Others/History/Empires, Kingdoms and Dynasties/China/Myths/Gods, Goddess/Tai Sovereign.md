# Tai Sovereign

The Tai Sovereign, also known as the Human Soverign (Chinese: 人皇), was the third legendary Chinese king after [[Pangu]]'s era. According to [[Yiwen Leiju]], he was the third and last of the [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors|Three Sovereigns]]
