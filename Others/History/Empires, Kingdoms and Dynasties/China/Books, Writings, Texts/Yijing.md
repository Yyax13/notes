# Yijing

The Yijing (Chinese: 易經) or I Ching, commonly translated as Book of Changes or Classic of Changes, is an ancient Chinese divination text that is among the oldest of the Chinese classics. The Yijing was originally a divination manual in the Occidental Zhou period (c. 1000 BCE - 750 BCE).
