# Emperor Shun

Emperor Shun (Chinese: 帝舜) was a leader of ancient china, regarded by some sources as the last of the Five Emperors among the [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors]]. According to the tradition, people with the surname Hu (Chinese: 胡) are descendants of Emperor Shun.

His predecessor is [[Emperor Yao]] while his sucessor is [[Yu The Great]] (credited with founding the Xia Dynasty and stopping the [[The Great Flood of Gun-Yu|Great Flood]]).

In later centuries, [[Emperor Yao]] and Shun were glorified for their virtue by Confucian philosophers. Shun was particularly renowned for his modesty and filial piety (xiao 孝).
