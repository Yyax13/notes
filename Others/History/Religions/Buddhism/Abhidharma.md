# Abhidharma

The _Abhidharma_ (lit./literally "about the _[[The Buddhist Dharma|dharma]]_") is a class of Buddhist texts, written in Sanskrit, the first of which data from the 3rd century CE. The Abhidharma texts contain detailed scholastic presentations of Buddhist doctrine, including doctrines that appear in [canonical Buddhist scriptures and commentaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripi%E1%B9%ADaka "Tripiṭaka").
