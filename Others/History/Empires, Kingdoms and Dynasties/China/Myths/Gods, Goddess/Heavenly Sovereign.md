# Heavenly Sovereign

The Heavenly Sovereign was the first legendary Chinese king after [[Pangu]]'s era.
According to [[Yiwen Leiju]] and Records of the Grand Historian, he was one of the legendary [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors]].
