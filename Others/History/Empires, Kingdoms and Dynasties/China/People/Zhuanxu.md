# Zhuanxu

Zhuanxu (Chinese: 颛顼), also known as Gaoyang (Chinese: 高阳), was a mythological emperor of ancient China.

In the account recorded by [[Sima Qian]], the [[Shiji]], Zhuanxu was a grandson of the [[Yellow Emperor]].

The [[Shiji]], Sovereign series and Zizhi tongjian waiji also credited Zhuanxu as one of the [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors|Five Emperors]].

His predecessor is [[Shaohao]] while his sucessor is [[Emperor Ku]].
