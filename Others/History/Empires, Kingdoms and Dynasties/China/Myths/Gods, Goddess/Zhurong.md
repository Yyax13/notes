# Zhurong

Zhu Rong (Chinese: 祝融), also known as Chongli (Chinese: 重黎).
According to the [[Huainanzi]], Zhurong is the god of fire and of the south.

According to the 1st variation of Baihu Tongyi (白虎通義), Zhurong is one of the legendary [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors]].
