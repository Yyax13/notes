# Nüwa

Nüwa, also read Nügua, is a mother goddess, culture hero and/or member of the [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors|Three Sovereigns]] of Chinese Mythology. She is a goddess in Chinese folk religion,  Chinese Buddhism, Confucianism and Taoism. She is credited with creating the humanity and repairing the Pillar of Heaven.
